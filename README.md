    <p>
      <em>Born From Goodbye</em> is not just a story—it's a soul spoken out loud.
      Tshepang Donald Molete was born on January 27th, 2001—just 22 days after his father passed away.
      For nearly two decades, he lived without knowing the truth about where he came from, or who he truly was.
      Raised in silence, pushed into independence too soon, and surrounded by unanswered questions, Tshepang began to search.
      What he found was a legacy of pain—but also, the seeds of purpose.
      This book is the first time his truth meets the world.
